Australian food manufacturing · 2006 to 2025

From Farm Gate to Supermarket Shelf

How Australia's food factories grew, where they work and who buys.

Almost everything in your trolley passed through a factory. Food factories are now Australia's biggest manufacturing industry. This story follows what they make and where. It ends with where the food goes and what we buy.

231,491 people worked in food factories in June 2025
22% of all factory value added came from food in 2024–25
$41.6 billion of manufactured food was sold overseas in 2024–25
Explore the story ↓

01 / Food pulls ahead

Food Pulls Ahead

Car plants closed and steel mills cut back. Food factories kept growing almost every year. Today they add more value than any other factory type.

Key finding

Food factories more than doubled. Their value added rose 111% since 2006–07. All other factories grew just 27%.

Key finding

Food is now the largest slice. Its share rose from 15% to 22%. Metals fell from 14% to 7%.

02 / Inside the factory gates

What Our Food Factories Make

Food manufacturing is really eight industries. They differ in size, workers and direction. Each keeps the same colour from here on.

  • 🥩Meat productsAbattoirs, sausages, bacon
  • 🍞Bakery productsBread, cakes, biscuits
  • 🧀Dairy productsMilk, cheese, butter, powder
  • 🍬Sugar and confectionerySugar, chocolate, lollies
  • 🍎Fruit and vegetablesCanned, frozen, juiced
  • 🌾Grain and cereal productsFlour, rice, cereal
  • 🐟SeafoodFish and prawn processing
  • 🥫Other food productsOils, sauces, ready meals

Key finding

Meat adds a third of the value. It makes 35% of food value added. Bakeries come third with 15%.

Key finding

Dairy and other food products grew the most. Dairy workers rose 45% and other food products 73%. Fruit, vegetable and seafood jobs almost halved.

Key finding

Bakeries employ the most people. They ranked first every year but one. Meat briefly took the lead in 2020.

Key finding

Cheese is near a record high. Factories made 431 thousand tonnes in 2024–25. Butter and whole milk powder fell sharply.

03 / Close to the farm

Where Food Is Made

Food factories sit close to the farms they rely on. That puts many of them in country towns. Some states depend on them far more than others.

Key finding

Tasmania relies on food the most. 32% of its factory workers make food. Across Australia the figure is about 20%.

Key finding

Victoria makes two thirds of our milk. Its three regions produced 67% in 2025. Dairy farm numbers fell 17% since 2021.

Key finding

Three inland river regions dominate. They crushed 71% of reported tonnes. Western Australia, outside this map, crushed only 2%.

04 / Beyond our shores

Where Our Food Goes

Food exports grew by three quarters in nine years. They reached $41.6 billion in 2024–25. Most of it heads north into Asia.

Key finding

The United States buys the most. It bought $8.3 billion, nine tenths of it meat. China and Japan come second and third.

05 / In the trolley

What Ends Up in Our Trolley

Factories follow what shoppers buy. Here is how our buying changed in five years.

Key finding

Plant-based foods grew the fastest. We bought 31% more per person. We bought less sugar, fruit and fats.

In short

A Quiet Giant

Food is now Australia's largest manufacturing industry. Its value has doubled since 2006–07. It keeps country towns working, from Gippsland to the Riverland. And it feeds shoppers here and across the world.

Notes on the data

Food industries follow the Australian and New Zealand Standard Industrial Classification. Oil and fat manufacturing is grouped with other food products. Dollar values are not adjusted for inflation. Small states such as the Northern Territory have larger survey errors. Dairy and wine region positions are approximate region centres. Export figures for 2025–26 are preliminary and are not shown.
