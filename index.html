Australia stopped making cars.
So what does it still make?

Almost everything in your trolley passed through a factory. Those factories are now our biggest manufacturing industry. This is the story of what they make, and where.

01 / The surprise

The Factory That Kept Growing

Car plants closed and steel mills shrank. Yet food factories more than doubled the value they add. Today they are the biggest slice of all manufacturing.

Key finding

Food left every other factory behind. Food factories grew 111%. All other factories grew just 27%. Others barely moved until 2021.

Key finding

Food is now number one. Food rose from 15% to 22%. Metals fell from 14% to 7%.

So what exactly are these food factories making?

02 / Inside the factory gates

Meat Makes the Money, Bakeries Make the Jobs

Food manufacturing is really eight industries. Some earn the most. Others employ the most people. Each keeps the same colour from here on.

  • Meat productsAbattoirs, sausages, bacon
  • Bakery productsBread, cakes, biscuits
  • Dairy productsMilk, cheese, butter, powder
  • Sugar and confectionerySugar, chocolate, lollies
  • Fruit and vegetablesCanned, frozen, juiced
  • Grain and cereal productsFlour, rice, cereal
  • SeafoodFish and prawn processing
  • Other food productsOils, sauces, ready meals

Key finding

Meat makes the money. Meat adds a third of the value, at 35%. Bakeries add only 15%. Yet bakeries employ almost as many people.

Key finding

Growth was far from even. Other food products grew 73% and dairy grew 45%. Fruit and vegetable processing lost 47% of its workers. Seafood lost 45%.

Key finding

Bakeries make the jobs. Bakeries employ the most people. They ranked first in every year but one. Meat led only in 2020.

Key finding

Cheese is winning. Cheese is close to a record high. Factories made 431 thousand tonnes by June 2025. Whole milk powder fell 88% from its peak.

These factories need farms close by. So where are they?

03 / Close to the farm

Where Food Is Made

Food factories sit near the farms they rely on. That makes them vital to country towns. Some states depend on them far more than others.

Key finding

Tasmania leans on food the most. 32% of its factory workers make food. Across Australia it is about 20%.

Key finding

Victoria is the dairy state. Its three regions made 67% of our milk. Dairy farm numbers fell 17% since 2021.

Key finding

Most wine grapes grow inland. Three river regions crushed 71% of tonnes. Famous names like the Barossa crush far less.

Much of this food never reaches an Australian table.

04 / Beyond our shores

Made Here, Eaten Overseas

Food exports grew from $24.0 billion to $41.6 billion. That is a rise of three quarters in nine years. Meat leads, and most of it heads north.

Key finding

America is our biggest customer. The United States buys $8.3 billion, nine tenths of it meat. Pick dairy from the menu and China takes first place.

At home, our own shopping is changing too.

05 / In the trolley

What We Put in the Trolley

Factories follow what shoppers buy. In five years we bought more snacks and plant-based foods. We bought less fruit, sugar and dairy.

Key finding

Plant-based food is on the rise. We bought 31% more plant-based milk and meat. Sugar fell 10% and fruit fell 7%.

So what does Australia still make?

Mostly, It Makes Food

Food is now Australia's largest manufacturing industry. It keeps country towns working, from Gippsland to the Riverland. It feeds shoppers here and buyers across the world. Next time you fill a trolley, think of the factory behind it.

Notes on the data

Australian financial years run from July to June, so "2025" on a chart means the year to June 2025. Food industries follow the Australian and New Zealand Standard Industrial Classification. Oil and fat manufacturing is grouped with other food products. Dollar values are not adjusted for inflation. Small states such as the Northern Territory have larger survey errors. Dairy and wine region positions are approximate region centres. Export figures for the year to June 2026 are preliminary and are not shown.
